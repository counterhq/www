Counter lets advertisers, agencies and publishers buy and sell media using their own
buying strategies. You decide which impressions to buy and what to pay, based on the
outcomes that matter to your business, and you can see how every decision is made.

## How Counter works

Media buying happens at two speeds, so Counter has two systems.

**System 1: models decide.** Every impression needs a decision in milliseconds. Your
strategies and models run inside the bidstream, close to the auction, on Counter's Rust
auction core. Each one answers a narrow question: should we bid, what is this impression
worth, and what should we pay?

**System 2: agents manage.** Campaigns run for days, weeks or months. An always-on agent
watches performance, investigates changes, adjusts budgets, runs experiments and deploys
new versions of your strategies. Use Counter's agent, or connect your own through
[MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) and the API.

Outcomes such as wins, clicks and sales flow back from System 1 to System 2, so each
result can inform the next decision.

## Who it's for

- **Advertisers** use sales, qualified leads or store visits to guide spend, with brand
  requirements built into buying decisions.
- **Agencies** turn their expertise into buying products, reuse strategies across
  clients and keep the models they develop.
- **Publishers** bring audience, context and placement signals into the decision, with
  rules for how their supply and signals are used.

## Open by design

- **Open bidder.** You can see the logic that buys your media.
- **Open models.** Start with a Counter model, adapt it, or bring your own.
- **Open APIs.** Everything in the platform is programmable, by people or agents.
- **Built on ARTF.** Counter uses the [IAB Tech Lab's Agentic RTB Framework](https://iabtechlab.com/standards/artf/)
  to run decisioning inside the bidstream instead of inventing a proprietary format.

Your models, data and logs stay yours. Accessing or exporting your own logs costs nothing extra.
