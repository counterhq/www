We are building Counter so advertisers, agencies and publishers can bring their
own data and expertise into how media is bought. You decide what an impression
is worth based on the outcomes that matter to your business.

For advertisers, that means using sales, qualified leads or store visits to guide
spend, with brand requirements built into buying decisions. Agencies can turn
their expertise into buying products, reuse strategies across campaigns and keep
the models they develop. Publishers can bring audience, context and placement
signals into the decision, helping buyers recognise the value of their inventory.

Start with a Counter model or bring your own. Your models and data remain yours.
You can inspect how decisions are made and access or export your logs without an
additional fee. Open APIs let you build your own workflows around the platform.

Underneath, a Rust auction core runs bidding logic close to the auction, reducing
the need to move requests between systems. APIs and MCP let agents operate the
platform, so teams can automate routine work while keeping rules and approvals
explicit.

the first campaigns on Counter.
