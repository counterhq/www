( 01 / 03 ) Why

Programmatic should be open. Today, buyers and publishers both give up too much control to the platforms between them.

We want to change that.

[01]

For brands

Own the logic that decides where your money goes.

Bring your data and models, understand every decision, and keep the intelligence you build.

[02]

For agencies

Build differentiated buying strategies rather than operating someone else's algorithm.

Create your own models, agents and commercial products and use them across clients.

[03]

For publishers

Make your inventory available through a clear, direct path to buyers.

Control how your supply is exposed, contribute your own signals and models, and remove unnecessary intermediaries between you and demand.

[01]

Open infrastructure

The core auction and bidding infrastructure is open source.

Publishers can inspect it, run it themselves or use the managed platform.

Buyers can inspect exactly how their decisions are executed.

Nobody needs to trust a closed black box.

[02]

Open models

Start with open models for common media decisioning problems, then train or fine-tune them on your own data.

Models can come from advertisers, agencies, publishers or independent developers.

The intelligence belongs to whoever creates it.

[03]

Your agents

Use our always-on campaign agent or bring your own.

Manage campaigns from Claude, ChatGPT or another agent while Counter handles the persistent execution underneath.

[04]

Direct connections

Create a clearer path between buyer and publisher.

Counter can work through existing infrastructure where necessary, but the goal is simple: fewer unnecessary hops, less reselling and understandable provenance for every impression.

[05]

Clear economics

Everyone should understand how money moves.

Advertisers can see what they are paying for.

Agencies can create explicit technology, optimisation and model fees.

Publishers can understand the route demand takes to their inventory.

[06]

Built for efficiency

Programmatic processes enormous volumes of data.

Waste less compute → lower cost → lower latency → lower carbon.

( 03 / 03 ) FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something we haven’t covered? Join the waitlist and tell us what you need.

01What are you building?

An open execution platform for programmatic media.

Buyers bring their own models and agents. Publishers bring their supply, signals and rules. Counter connects both sides directly and gives them an open place to execute.

02Is this a DSP or an SSP?

It overlaps with both, but we are not trying to recreate either in the traditional sense.

We are building an open layer for media decisioning and execution.

03What is open source?

The core auction and bidding infrastructure is open source.

We also release an open family of media decisioning models, along with the tools and examples needed to run them.

04Do I need to build my own models?

No.

You can start with open models provided by the community and fine-tune them using your own data.

More advanced teams can bring completely custom models.

05Can I train models on the platform?

Yes.

The platform includes infrastructure for training, evaluating, fine-tuning, testing and deploying models into live buying.

06Where do AI agents fit?

Agents manage the longer-running work around a campaign.

They can monitor performance, adjust settings, run experiments, retrain models, investigate problems and produce reports.

You can use our agent or connect your own.

07Can I use Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes.

The platform is API-first, so agents are native operators rather than an interface added afterwards.

You can manage a campaign from Claude, ChatGPT or another compatible agent.

For example:

“Check this campaign every morning. If CPA rises above £20, investigate why and tell me before making a major change.”
08What happens to my logs?

They are yours.

Auction logs, model decisions, campaign activity and agent actions are available to you without additional logging or data-access charges.

09What does direct supply mean?

Every impression should have a clear path back to the publisher.

Supply can still come through infrastructure partners, but we want to avoid unnecessary reselling and opaque supply chains.

10How can agencies make money?

Transparency does not mean agencies cannot make margin.

Platform fees, optimisation fees, model fees and performance-based economics can all be explicitly built into campaigns.

11When will it be available?

We are building the first version now.

We are looking for agencies, brands, publishers, developers and adtech teams interested in helping shape it.
